# 🚀 Quick Start: Emergency Login

## For New Students (Registration)

### Step-by-Step Registration

1. **Open the Application**
   - Navigate to the home page
   - Click "Register (New Student)"

2. **Stage 1: Generate Your Student ID**
   - Select your Batch Year (e.g., 2024)
   - Choose your Department (e.g., Computer Science)
   - Select your Section (A, B, C, etc.)
   - Enter your Student Number (001-999)
   - Click "Proceed to Stage 2"
   - **Note your generated Student ID** (e.g., `24CS1001`)

3. **Stage 2: Enter Your Details**
   - Full Name
   - Email address
   - Phone number (optional)
   - Gender
   - Contact information (optional)
   - Check "I agree to terms and conditions"
   - Click "Proceed to Stage 3"

4. **Stage 3: Set Emergency Password** ⚠️ **IMPORTANT**
   - Your username will be your Student ID
   - Create a strong password:
     - At least 6 characters
     - Must have letters AND numbers
     - Example: `john2024`, `mary123abc`
   - Confirm your password
   - **Remember this password!** It's for emergency login
   - Click "Proceed to Face Capture"

5. **Stage 4: Capture Your Face**
   - Allow camera access
   - Position your face in the center
   - Ensure good lighting
   - Click "Capture"
   - Crop your face if needed
   - Click "Confirm & Register"
   - ✅ Registration Complete!

## For Existing Students (Login)

### Method 1: Face Authentication (Primary) 🔐

1. Go to "Face Authentication" page
2. Ensure "Face Authentication (Primary)" is selected
3. Click camera to capture your face
4. Crop if needed
5. Click "Verify Face"
6. ✅ Logged in!

### Method 2: Emergency Login (Backup) 🆘

**When to use:**
- Wearing a mask
- Poor lighting
- Camera not working
- Face recognition fails

**Steps:**
1. Go to "Face Authentication" page
2. Select "🆘 Emergency Login (Username & Password)"
3. Enter your **Student ID** as username
   - Example: `24CS1001`
4. Enter your **emergency password**
   - The password you created during registration
5. Click "🔓 Login"
6. ✅ Logged in!

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Forgot Password
**Problem:** Can't remember emergency password

**Solution:**
1. Try face authentication instead
2. If face doesn't work, contact administrator
3. Admin can reset your password

### Scenario 2: Face Not Recognized
**Problem:** Face authentication confidence too low

**Solution:**
1. Try again with better lighting
2. Remove obstructions (glasses, mask)
3. Use emergency login as backup

### Scenario 3: Camera Not Working
**Problem:** Camera permission denied or not available

**Solution:**
1. Use emergency login instead
2. Enter Student ID and password
3. Access granted without camera

### Scenario 4: Wearing Mask
**Problem:** Face is covered, can't use face authentication

**Solution:**
1. Switch to emergency login
2. Use Student ID and password
3. No need to remove mask

## Tips & Best Practices

### For Registration
- ✅ Write down your Student ID immediately
- ✅ Choose a memorable but strong password
- ✅ Store password securely (password manager)
- ✅ Ensure good lighting for face capture
- ✅ Remove glasses/hat for face registration

### For Login
- ✅ Try face authentication first (faster)
- ✅ Use emergency login as backup
- ✅ Keep your password confidential
- ✅ Don't share your Student ID with others
- ✅ Report any login issues to admin

### Security
- ❌ Don't share your password
- ❌ Don't use simple passwords (123456, password)
- ❌ Don't write password on paper
- ✅ Use password manager
- ✅ Change password if compromised
- ✅ Log out after use

## Troubleshooting

### Registration Issues

**Problem:** "Student ID already exists"
- **Cause:** ID already registered
- **Solution:** Contact admin to check/delete old record

**Problem:** "Invalid password"
- **Cause:** Password doesn't meet requirements
- **Solution:** Use 6+ characters with letters AND numbers

**Problem:** "No face detected"
- **Cause:** Face not visible in camera
- **Solution:** Improve lighting, center face, try again

### Login Issues

**Problem:** "Invalid Student ID or password"
- **Cause:** Wrong credentials
- **Solution:** 
  - Check Student ID format (e.g., 24CS1001)
  - Verify password (case-sensitive)
  - Try face authentication instead

**Problem:** "No matching face found"
- **Cause:** Face not in database or poor match
- **Solution:**
  - Ensure you're registered
  - Try emergency login
  - Contact admin if persistent

**Problem:** "Confidence too low"
- **Cause:** Face match below 75% threshold
- **Solution:**
  - Improve lighting
  - Remove obstructions
  - Try multiple times
  - Use emergency login
